ROB-01Robotics · 2024 to present

Robot and vision integration on 4 photovoltaic lines

ABB, Estun and Epson SCARA robots working with machine vision, servo drives and EtherCAT on complete solar module lines across the MENA region, Europe and Asia.

From factory acceptance at headquarters to start-up on customer sites, including a 500 MW solar module plant.

ABB, Estun, Epson SCARAVision-guided pick and placeCognex, Keyence, HikrobotBeckhoff TwinCAT 3EtherCAT

View details

I program the ABB and Estun robots for vision-guided pick and place and set up their communication with the PLC: handshakes, part data and fault handling. I calibrate the cameras and inspection routines, program the PLC sequences in TwinCAT 3 and configure the EtherCAT networks and servo drives. When a line stops, I trace the cause across robot, PLC, drives, vision and power supply and turn the fix into a standard for similar installations. On one line I integrated a UPS to keep the servo drive control circuits powered through mains outages.

PLC-02PLC and design · 2020

Weighing, labeling and packing line: PLC and HMI design

Final-year engineering project with a teammate at a poultry plant. The line ran on hard-wired logic and a failing controller whose spare boards were no longer sold, so we redesigned its control around a Siemens S7-1200 PLC with a new HMI.

Siemens S7-1200TIA PortalGRAFCETLadderHMISolidWorks

View details

The line weighs crates of chicken, rejects any crate outside the weight window, labels the good ones and packs them in net. I wrote the GRAFCET sequence and the I/O list and programmed the PLC: the load cell signal is scaled with NORM_X and SCALE_X, then IN_RANGE and OUT_RANGE blocks accept or reject each crate. I built the HMI with line, alarm and control screens (continuous and single cycle, start, stop and emergency stop), designed the mechanics in SolidWorks with the conveyor motor sizing and tested the whole system.

MNT-07Maintenance · 2017

Preventive maintenance plan for automated machines

Final-year project at ISET Jendouba in the maintenance department of SO.TU.LIN in El Kef, a wet wipes manufacturer preparing for ISO 9001 certification.

FMECAPareto analysisServo drivesPneumaticsPreventive maintenance

View details

I applied FMECA to four production machines: the large and small format cutting machines, the packaging machine and the lid applicator. There was no breakdown history, so I explained the method to the maintenance technicians and rated each failure mode with them for frequency, severity and detection. I broke each machine down into its mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and hydraulic subsystems, ranked the critical components with a Pareto analysis and wrote a preventive maintenance plan with actions, intervals and spare parts, plus checklists for the technicians.
